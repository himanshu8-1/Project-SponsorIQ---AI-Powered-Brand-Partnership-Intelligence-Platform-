SponsorIQ – Brand Partnership Intelligence Platform
End-to-End Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Project
1. Project Overview

Today's sponsorship industry is highly fragmented. Companies invest crores in sponsorships, yet many decisions are driven by intuition, relationships, or limited historical data rather than a structured evaluation process.

There is no centralized intelligence platform that helps businesses answer questions such as:

Which brands are most likely to sponsor a particular event?
Which events best align with a brand's audience and marketing objectives?
Which industries are increasing sponsorship investments?
Which opportunities offer the highest potential ROI?
Who are the decision-makers within target organizations?
Which sponsorship opportunities deserve priority?

To address this problem, this project builds SponsorIQ, an end-to-end Brand Partnership Intelligence Platform that integrates multiple business datasets into a single analytics solution. The platform combines SQL, Power BI, ETL, DAX, and business intelligence techniques to help organizations make data-driven sponsorship decisions.

2. Business Problem

Organizations often face several challenges when identifying sponsorship opportunities:

Sponsorship information is scattered across multiple Excel files.
Decision-makers lack a centralized view of brands, events, budgets, and historical sponsorships.
Brand-event matching is performed manually.
Marketing teams spend significant time researching companies before outreach.
Sales teams lack visibility into pipeline status and follow-up progress.
Executives cannot easily identify high-potential sponsorship opportunities.

These limitations result in slower decision-making, inefficient outreach, and missed partnership opportunities.

3. Project Objective

The objective of SponsorIQ is to build a centralized Business Intelligence platform capable of:

Managing brand information
Tracking sponsorship history
Monitoring marketing investments
Managing CRM activities
Recommending suitable sponsorship opportunities
Providing executive-level dashboards for strategic decision-making

6. Database Design

Instead of connecting Excel directly to Power BI, all datasets are first migrated into MySQL.

This provides:

Better scalability
Data integrity
Primary Keys
Foreign Keys
Relational Modeling
Faster querying
Interview-ready architecture

The database follows a relational schema where Brand Master and Event Master serve as parent tables while CRM, Sponsorship History, Marketing Campaigns, and Brand-IP Fit are connected through foreign key relationships.

7. ETL Process
